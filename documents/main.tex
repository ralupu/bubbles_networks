\begin{abstract}
This paper studies systemic risk through time-varying networks constructed from overlapping financial bubble episodes.
We build (i) bubble-overlap networks from bubble episode timing and (ii) optional FRM networks from rolling-window quantile regression.
We analyze centrality dynamics and provide a forecasting workflow using temporal graph neural networks (TGNNs).
\end{abstract}
