\section{Introduction}

Financial bubbles can create concentrated periods of market stress and potential contagion across firms.
This work operationalizes contagion hypotheses via a time series of directed networks based on the temporal overlap of bubble episodes.
The empirical focus is on end-to-end reproducibility: code, data locations, figure generation, and paper build are all automated.

\paragraph{Contributions (code-aligned).}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A bubble-overlap network construction pipeline with reproducible artifacts and stable figure/table exports.
  \item Temporal network centrality analytics for identifying time-varying systemic importance.
  \item An optional FRM (quantile-regression) module and a TGNN forecasting pipeline.
\end{itemize}
